#Life Simulator (Python + Tkinter)


Life Simulator is a small interactive life management game built with Python and Tkinter GUI.
The player starts as an 18-year-old character with basic stats such as money, hunger, mood, intelligence, and charm.
Each day brings new random events, choices, and challenges that influence the player’s life path.

🎮 Features

Dynamic events: Random daily situations that affect your stats and choices.

Jobs & Housing: Work your way up from an unemployed renter to a wealthy villa owner.

Mood & Hunger system: Maintain balance to survive longer.

Leaderboards: Records your best day survived and maximum wealth.

Simple GUI: Built entirely using Python’s standard Tkinter library — no external dependencies.

🧠 How to Play

Run python your_filename.py

Enter your name and start the game.

Choose wisely in each event to manage your stats.

Survive as many days as possible without going bankrupt or losing motivation.

1. Start the Game
Enter your name.
You begin with:
Age 18
$800
Basic rental room
Random starting charm & intelligence

2. Daily Cycle
Each in-game day contains:
3 events, each offering 2–3 choices.
After the 3 events, you must eat a meal (food restores hunger and mood).

3. Events System
Events include:
✅ Random daily events
✅ Job opportunities
✅ House upgrades or downgrades
✅ Training and social encounters
Many events have conditions, such as:
Intelligence ≥ 50
Charm ≥ 70
Specific house rank
Choices impact your attributes:
Money
Mood
Hunger
Charm
Intelligence
Job
Salary
Housing

4. Attributes

Your survival depends on key stats:
Attribute	Meaning
Money 💰	Needed for rent, food, and events
Hunger 🍞	Drops daily, and you die if it reaches 0
Mood 😊	Mental state; hitting 0 causes depression (game over)
Intelligence 🧠	Unlocks better jobs and events
Charm 💖	Helps social events and special rewards
House 🏠	Impacts mood and rent
Salary 💵	Earned daily if you have a job
5. Food System

After every 3 events, you must choose a meal:
Bread → cheap, low recovery
Fast Food → balanced
Deluxe Meal → expensive, best recovery
Skipping food is impossible, and low hunger can kill you.

6. Survival Conditions

You lose the game if:
❌ Money reaches 0 → Bankruptcy
❌ Hunger reaches 0 → Starvation
❌ Mood reaches 0 → Depression
The game then records your:
Most days survived
Highest money earned

Best player
